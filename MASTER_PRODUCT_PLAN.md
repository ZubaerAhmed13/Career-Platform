# CareerOS AI — Product plan

**Vision:** one trustworthy career workspace with verified personal career facts reused across applications without rewriting the master profile.

**Audience:** students, graduates, career changers and experienced international applicants.

**Planned modules:** Career Intelligence Vault (facts/evidence), Resume Studio (templates, tailored versions and optional photos), Cover Letter Studio (natural editable writing), Job Analysis (requirements/fact mapping), Application Tracker (real timelines/document snapshots), Interview Academy (application-grounded practice), Job Discovery (verified vacancies), Career Development (skills/growth), and intelligent Automation (consented reminders).

**Differentiators:** evidence-first career facts, editable human-sounding output, versioned application records, privacy-first international profiles, transparent AI limitations and native English/German foundations. Generative features must never invent experience or claim AI output is verified.

## Milestone 2 boundary
The verified Career Vault becomes the source of truth for resume versions, cover letters and applications. Master facts and their evidence stay independent of derived, job-targeted documents. Users explicitly approve imported and AI-suggested content. An actual file-processing pipeline is still a future release dependency.
